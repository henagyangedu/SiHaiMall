[简体中文](授权说明.md) | **English**

SihaiMall's source code is public. The author is happy to share it for learning and personal use, and asks those who run a business on it to pay a one-time license fee that funds maintenance and further development. So it is free for noncommercial use and paid for commercial use:

- License: [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](../LICENSE.md)
- Commercial use: requires a commercial license at the prices below

> This page is a plain-language explanation. **What is legally binding is the text of `LICENSE.md` and the license certificate or contract confirmed when you buy a commercial license.** This page is a translation of the [Chinese version](授权说明.md); if the two differ, the Chinese version applies. Prices and terms may change; what applies is the Chinese version at the time of purchase, and licenses already bought are not affected by later changes.

## 1. What you can do for free

No need to contact the author:

- Personal study, research, experiments, school projects
- Running a hobby site that makes no money
- Internal use by charities, schools, research institutions, government bodies and other nonprofit organizations
- Evaluating whether the system suits you: trying it, testing it, comparing it with alternatives, setting up and adjusting a store before it opens for business
- Reading and modifying the code, and sharing your modified version with others (keep the license and copyright notice; recipients are likewise limited to noncommercial use)

## 2. What counts as commercial use

You need a commercial license before you:

- Run a store that sells goods or services with it, whatever its size
- Use it in the course of business as a company or sole trader
- Build, customize or deploy it for a client for a fee (one license per domain the client ends up using, see section 5)
- Sell or rent it, or a modified version, as part of a product or service, or offer paid hosting based on it (standard prices do not apply to this; please get in touch)

If you are not sure whether your use is commercial, ask first.

## 4. What a license includes

- Commercial use of the system on the licensed domain, **forever**.
- The right to modify the code and build on it for your own store.
- **Free upgrades**: every version published after your purchase, at no extra cost.
- A license certificate stating the domain, the license type and the licensee.

**Not included**: installation, deployment, custom development, technical support and training. Contact the author separately if you need them.

**Not allowed**: reselling or lending the license to someone else; selling the source code or a modified version as a product.

**Agencies building stores for clients**: each client's store needs one license, of the type that matches the client (the end user) and its sales. The agency may buy them together and get the volume discount.

## 6. Other terms

- **Outgrowing your tier**: once annual sales exceed the Basic limit, pay the difference to move to Plus before the next calendar year begins. You pay only the difference between the two tiers.
- **Changing domain**: a license can be moved to another domain, free of charge once per year per license. The old domain is no longer licensed afterwards. Contact the author for an updated certificate.
- **Refunds**: no refunds once the certificate has been issued. You can try and evaluate the system for free beforehand, so please make sure it suits you before buying.
- **Honest declaration**: you choose the license type and tier according to your actual situation; the author will not audit your books. If the choice is clearly wrong (for example a company holding an Individual license), the author may ask you to pay the difference.
- **Invoices and receipts**: ask at the time of purchase if you need one.

## 7. How to buy

Open an issue in this repository with "Commercial license" (or “商业授权”) in the title. You do not need to post any business details, just a way to reach you. English is fine.

You will be asked for: the domain to license, the licensee's name (a person's name or a company name), and the license type.

**Steps**

1. Contact the author and agree on the license type, domain and amount.
2. The author sends you the payment details and you pay.
3. The author issues an electronic license certificate carrying a license number. The license takes effect when you receive it.

**Payment**

- USD: PayPal. The author sends you a PayPal invoice; pay that invoice, and please do not use "Friends and Family".
- CNY: Alipay.

Account details are given to you directly after you get in touch. **They are never written in any file in the repository.**

**Beware of impersonation**

The source code is public, so anyone can copy it and change the text. Please note:

- **The only official repository is <https://github.com/henagyangedu/SiHaiMall>.** Only the author reached through that repository can issue commercial licenses.
- Payment codes or account details you see on any other website, repository or chat group do not come from the author. Do not pay them.
- Every certificate has a unique license number. If you doubt a certificate, open an issue in the official repository and ask the author to check that the number matches the domain. You only need to give the number.
- Licenses "transferred" or "bought on your behalf" by a third party are not valid.

A sample certificate is available in Chinese: [授权书模板](授权书模板.md).
